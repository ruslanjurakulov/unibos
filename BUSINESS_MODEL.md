# Business model — hypotheses
Subscriptions and NFC hardware first; paid add-ons and extra branches later. Proposed UZS/month: Free 0 (20 products, 3 tables); Starter 99k (menu, QR/NFC, call waiter); Business 249k (orders, cashier, kitchen, analytics, certified payment/fiscal when ready); Pro 499k (future inventory/finance/staff). Optional stock 99k, finance 99k, staff 49k, loyalty 49k, AI 79k, branch 149k. Not final prices or currently sold features. Avoid duplicate charges for bundled modules.

Super admin manages versioned plans, existing module entitlements, limits and promos without deploy; cannot create unimplemented functionality. Server validates every protected endpoint; expiry preserves data and exports with defined grace and read-only policy. Subscription webhook verification, cancellation, renewal and proration rules. Restaurant revenue settles directly to restaurant merchant where supported; UniBOS subscription revenue separate. Hardware costs, support, provider fees, taxes and refunds measured. Validate willingness to pay with 5–10 pilots before locking tariffs.
