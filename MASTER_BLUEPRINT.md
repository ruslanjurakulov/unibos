# UniBOS — Master Blueprint v1.0
Status: DRAFT — founder approval required | 2026-11-15

## Vision
Unified Business Operating System. One platform. Your entire business. Uzbekistan-first, global-ready. Initial vertical: restaurants and cafes; no coupling of the core to restaurant-only entities. Domain unibos.uz purchased via Billur for UZS 27,000/year; renewal 2027-11-15. Domain registration is not trademark clearance or deployment.

## Product surfaces
Public marketing and interactive demo; tenant workspace; anonymous guest QR/NFC menu and ordering; staff operations (waiter, cashier, kitchen); platform super admin. Mobile, tablet and desktop. Uzbek/Russian initially proposed; English localization foundation. No fake customer counts or testimonials.

## MVP scope
Tenant onboarding, branches, identity/roles, menu CRUD, optional import with human review, tables and revocable QR/NFC URLs, guest cart/orders, kitchen statuses, waiter/bill requests, cashier cash recording, daily sales reports, configurable subscription entitlements, admin audit. Online payments and fiscal receipts only after provider agreements, sandbox and compliance verification. Guest registration optional. Inventory/recipes/suppliers, finance, loyalty, payroll and AI extensions after pilot; architecture anticipates them. No video surveillance in this project baseline.

## Decision gates
Founder approves blueprint, brand, hosting and budget. Legal data residency, payment/fiscal certification, trademark and merchant onboarding unresolved. No production deployment, purchase or external publication without authorization. See linked files in README.
